Four things worth knowing before you scroll

  • Ctrl+D duplicates the whole image — it does not deselect, unlike Photoshop. Deselect is Shift+Ctrl+A.
  • Ctrl+E re-exports to your last-used file and format. It does not fit the image in the window — that's Shift+Ctrl+J — a mix-up common in older guides.
  • Ctrl+I inverts the selection, not the image's colors. Colors → Invert has no default shortcut of its own.
  • Every binding below is a GIMP default. Your setup may differ if you've customized things — check Edit → Keyboard Shortcuts (GIMP → Keyboard Shortcuts on Mac) for your actual bindings, or to assign new ones (Ctrl+Alt+key is rarely already taken).
